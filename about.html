What Is OPSEC?

Operational Security (OPSEC) is the process of protecting your personal information from hackers and other malicious attackers.

Why It Matters

Some people think, "I have nothing to hide, so why should I care?" But privacy still matters. Most people would not want strangers knowing all of their personal information. Privacy is about having control over what people know about you.

Bad OPSEC can be dangerous. According to Security.org, identity theft happens very often in the United States. Many people have had social media accounts hacked or personal information stolen online.

Our Mission

OPSEC Today was created to help everyday computer users learn how to stay safe online. Many internet users do not realize how much personal information is collected every day.

Data brokers and cybercriminals can collect, store, and even sell user data. Our goal is to teach people how to protect their privacy and take control of their information online.
