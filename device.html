Lock Down Your Device

Your operating system is the main part of your own OPSEC. Windows and macOS are the most popular operating systems in the world. With that though, they are also among the most targeted. In order to protect yourself, you won't just have to change settings, but also change how you use your computer. This section has settings you should switch today to bolster your device's OPSEC

Settings Comparison: Windows vs macOS

The table below shows where to find the most important OPSEC settings on each system.

Category Windows macOS
Tracking Make sure diagnostic data is set to required only. Turn off ALL tailored experiences. Turn off share mac analytics and also turn off "improve siri”
Software updates Keep automatic updates on. Hackers rely on you not updating and exposing old vulnerabilities. Turn on automatic updates, including ALL security responses.
Login Privacy Use a strong pin or even a long password. Make sure you hide account details on sign in page. Use a local account instead of a Microsoft account. Use a strong password as well and turn on FileVault. Disk encryption is a must.
App Permissions Review camera, microphone, location, and contacts. Revoke anything that doesn't need access. See who has accssess to your camera, microphone, location, full disk access, and screen recordings. Be thorough.
