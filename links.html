Useful Links

This page is a quick list of trusted tools and sites cybersecurity professionals use.

Check Yourself

These sites tell you what data is already out there about you and how trackable you are right now.

  • Have I Been Pwned — Type in your email and see every data breach it's been part of. If your password leaked, change it everywhere.
  • BrowserLeaks — Shows exactly what your browser is leaking right now: IP, location, fonts, screen size, WebRTC data, etc...
  • AmIUnique — Tests your browser fingerprint and tells you how unique you look to websites. The more unique, the easier you are to track.
  • What Is My IP Address — Quick check of your public IP and what location it points to. Useful for testing if your VPN is actually working.

Tools to Download

  • Bitwarden — Free password manager. Stop reusing passwords. This one app fixes a huge chunk of your security risk.
  • Signal — End-to-end encrypted messaging. Replaces texts and most messaging apps. Recommended by basically every security expert.
  • Tor Browser — Routes your traffic through three encrypted hops so no one (not even your ISP) knows what sites you're visiting.

Best Browser Extensions

If you're not switching browsers yet, at least add these.

  • uBlock Origin — The best ad and tracker blocker. Free, open source, no shady business model. Install this one even if you install nothing else.

Best VPNs

A VPN hides your IP address and your traffic from your ISP.

  • Mullvad VPN — Flat $5/month, no email needed to sign up, accepts cash. The most privacy respecting VPN you can buy. mullvad.net
  • Proton VPN — Made by the same team as ProtonMail. Has a real free tier if you can't pay. protonvpn.com
  • IVPN — Similar privacy stance to Mullvad. Audited and trustworthy. ivpn.net
